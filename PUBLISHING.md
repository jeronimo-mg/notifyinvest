# Publishing NotifyInvest to Uptodown

Since I cannot facilitate the automated upload due to browser limitations, please follow these steps to publish your app manually.

## 1. Prerequisites

You will need the following files, which are located in your project folder:

* **APK File:** `notifyinvest-v11-sources.apk` (Root folder)
* **App Icon:** `mobile/assets/images/icon.png` (512x512 or larger recommended)
* **Screenshots:** You can take screenshots of the app running on your device or emulator.

## 2. Steps to Publish

1. **Go to Uptodown Developers Zone:**
    Open [https://en.uptodown.com/developers-zone](https://en.uptodown.com/developers-zone) in your browser.

2. **Log In / Sign Up:**
    If you don't have an account, register as a developer. It's free.

3. **Add New App:**
    Click on the "Add new app" button in your console.

4. **Fill in App Details:**
    * **App Name:** NotifyInvest
    * **Package Name:** `com.notifyinvest.mobile` (This should be auto-detected from the APK)
    * **Category:** Finance (or similar)
    * **Description:**
        > NotifyInvest helps you track stock market signals and news.
        >
        > **New in Version 11:**
        > * **Source Filtering:** Select firmly which news sources you trust in Settings.
        > * **Enhanced Dashboard:** View source information directly in the signal list.
        > * **Direct Links:** "Source" buttons now clearly indicate the origin of the news.
    * **Icon:** Upload the `icon.png` file.
    * **Screenshots:** Upload at least 3 screenshots of your app.

5. **Upload APK:**
    Select and upload the `notifyinvest-v11-sources.apk` file.

6. **Submit:**
    Review your information and click "Publish" or "Submit". Uptodown's team will review your app (usually within 24-48 hours).

## 3. Post-Submission

* Check your email for status updates from Uptodown.
* Once published, you will get a public link to share with users.
